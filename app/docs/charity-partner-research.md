## Reconciliation and copy boundaries

- Separate dollars raised by Olympiad from grants distributed by the broader foundation.
- Separate announced grant cycles from fiscal-year figures. Cause IQ summarizes FY2025 distributions as over $816,000; this differs from the $750,000 cycle announcement. Review the underlying Form 990 and grant ledger before selecting a single annual total. No arithmetic combined total is recommended.
- Banner's November2025 $250,000 announcement is distinct evidence from the FY2025 aggregator's $75,000 Banner row; dates/periods may differ. Do not call either erroneous without reconciliation.
- The club's About page describes a 30% endowment / remainder general-fund allocation. Confirm current policy before putting percentages on the event site. Avoid 'every dollar goes directly to children' wording.
- Match legal entities carefully: Saguaros Children's Charities foundation EIN86-0815349 differs from The Scottsdale Saguaros club EIN88-3137259. Do not mix incoming gifts with outgoing grants or confuse the unrelated Saguaro Foundation.
- No 2027 grantee list, award amounts or grant-specific outcome measurements were established in this review.

## Recommended site treatment

Keep the eight featured partner cards with short mission descriptions and official links. Add a separate dated Giving in Action section: 2025 grant-cycle story, Banner access-to-care story, Care Fund housing stability story, and 2024 grants for education/youth services. Clearly label broader Saguaros giving. Link supporting articles beside each amount. No new claims have been deployed by this research pass.
