## Email copy
Hi there,

I’m inviting a small group of Saguaros members who are planning to bring a team to Olympiad 2027 to try our new team hub early.

The goal is simple: make it easier to get your business involved, organize your team, and know what comes next—all in one place.

**Start your team**
https://scottsdaleolympiad.com/#captain

Use the email address that received this invitation. You’ll receive a secure sign-in link, then you can:

- Add your business name, team name, industry, and captain contact details.
- Add a few teammates now—or save your roster and finish it later.
- Return to update contact information and shirt preferences.
- Choose whether your business appears in the participating-team directory.

**A quick refresher**
Olympiad brings businesses together for friendly competition in support of Arizona children’s charities. Each team has at least six participants, including the captain, and a $3,000 minimum fundraising goal. Industry cups recognize the top fundraisers; medals recognize the teams that win the games.

This is early access to team setup. Fundraising collection and live standings aren’t open yet, and there’s no payment to make in this pilot. We’ll share the confirmed 2027 date and event details as they’re finalized.

**Help us make it better**
After you try it, reply to this email with:

1. Was it easy to sign in and create your team?
2. Could you add a few teammates and return to your roster?
3. What felt confusing—or was missing?

A screenshot is helpful if you run into an issue. Please don’t forward your sign-in link; it’s personal to you.

Thanks for helping us make Olympiad easier for every captain and participant.

Sean Caldwell
Olympiad 2027 Chairman
The Saguaros
