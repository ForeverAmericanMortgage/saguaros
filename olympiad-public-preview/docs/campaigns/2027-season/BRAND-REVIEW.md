## What changed

- Actual current website cactus logo replaces a plain text brand header. Image proportions are preserved and an accessible brand name is included.
- Navy heading panel, cream page, sage supporting area and dark green buttons follow the current Olympiad design. The palette is a practical website alignment, not a claimed official brand-book specification.
- Cormorant Garamond headings and Poppins body copy have Georgia and Arial email fallbacks. Many inboxes will use the fallback fonts.
- Recruitment drafts use the real team photo from prior sent Mailchimp campaigns. A caption identifies it as a past event; it is not presented as 2027 photography.
- Roster/fundraising reminders omit the large photo to prioritize the task.
- Each draft has one principal CTA, chairman signoff, mission reminder and Mailchimp address/preferences/unsubscribe fields.
- Source campaign copy preserves the six-person minimum, $3,000 team minimum, industry fundraising cups and game medals.
- Old 2025/2026 event logos, dates, prizes, tax claims and offers are not carried into the 2027 drafts.

## Remaining brand gap

A formal Saguaros brand book was not located in the accessible Drive searches. The files titled “FAM Brand Book” and the generic Branding folder containing a Forever American Mortgage palette are a different organization and were excluded. The local app/docs/brand-guidelines.md calls itself derived guidance and contains older Barlow/Sora recommendations that disagree with current website fonts; it is not an official Saguaros source.

This review establishes a documented interim campaign standard from actual Saguaros assets and current public presentation. A formal book, if supplied or found later, should override unconfirmed rules for exact color values, minimum logo size, clear space and approved type hierarchy. Official Olympiad 2027 event lockup remains pending; current drafts use the evergreen Saguaros logo and plain-text event/year rather than relabeling old artwork.
