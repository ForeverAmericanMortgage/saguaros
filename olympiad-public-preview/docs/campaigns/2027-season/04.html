First, choose a captain. They’ll register your business, enter a team name and select your industry.

Next, start your roster. Include the captain and work toward at least six participants. Names, email addresses, phone numbers and shirt preferences help us keep everyone informed and prepared.

Finally, make a fundraising plan together. The minimum is $3,000 per team. You can build toward a larger stretch goal as the season progresses. Fundraising cups are awarded within industries; game medals are earned on the field.
