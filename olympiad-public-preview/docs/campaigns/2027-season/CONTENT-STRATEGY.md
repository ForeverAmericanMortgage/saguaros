# Olympiad 2027 content strategy

## Objective and season
Planning assumption: recruitment December 2026–February 2027; fundraising activation February–April 2027. Event date and registration/fundraising deadlines are not yet confirmed. Proposed windows are editorial planning, not send schedules.

**Main outcome:** approved business teams with complete rosters, then teams meeting the $3,000 minimum and choosing stretch goals. Public standings use verified fundraising only. Cups reward top fundraising by industry; medals reward game winners.

## What the prior season tells us
The connected Mailchimp account returned 15 campaigns total. Three sent campaigns cover Olympiad 2026, all in April. No December–February recruitment series was found in this account; another account or channel may contain earlier outreach. The two September 2026 early-access sends are 2027 pilot activity, not prior-season evidence.

## Editorial calendar
| Period | Priority | Default cadence | Stop/transition rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| December | Introduce the event, purpose, captain role | 2 recruitment emails | Stop recruitment when a team is created |
| January | Convert returning/new businesses; invite friendly rivals | 2 prospect emails; 1 separate captain referral email | Registered captains receive onboarding instead |
| February | Finish recruitment and activate committed teams | 1–2 prospect emails; 2 captain support emails | Pending teams receive review updates; approved teams start fundraising support once enabled |
| March | Practical fundraising tools, progress, impact | 2 core emails; targeted reminder only when needed | Split below $3,000, at minimum, and stretch-goal teams |
| April | Close genuine gaps and prepare for game day | Up to 1 relevant campaign/week near confirmed event | Stop fundraising asks when target/deadline reached; prioritize logistics |

Use a shared frequency cap: at most one marketing email per person per seven days, with a normal baseline of two per month. Event-critical operational notices are separate. Deduplicate captain/participant/club-member roles by email. Never send every draft to everyone.

## Audience journeys
1. Eligible prospect → Start team. Segment returning companies separately from businesses new to Olympiad.
2. Verified captain without team → Setup reminder; stop immediately after creation.
3. Pending review → Approval/status communication, not public-team or fundraising claims.
4. Approved team, incomplete roster → Saved progress plus precise missing steps. Stop when complete.
5. Approved team below minimum → One current product, one practical ask, contact for help.
6. Minimum achieved → Thank you, impact and optional stretch goal; no misleading below-minimum reminders.
7. Permissioned participants → What Olympiad is, how to help, event guide. A captain-entered roster is not marketing consent.
8. Declined, unsubscribed, cleaned, bounced or suppressed → Exclude from recruitment/campaign sends as applicable; never auto-resubscribe.

## Message and brand system
- One headline, one objective, one primary button; short mobile paragraphs.
- Current 2027 identity: deep green, warm ivory and restrained gold; real event/team photos when relevant.
- Consistent sender Sean Caldwell | The Saguaros; replies to scaldwell@saguaros.com.
- Teams are participating business sponsors. Avoid “find a sponsor” framing.
- Main CTA by stage: Start your team → Update roster → Fundraising guide → Verified standings → Event guide.
- Recruitment emails need a compact “six people minimum / $3,000 per team / industry cups and game medals” explanation.
- Historical photos and giving stories must be dated. Never imply a prior-year grant or product is guaranteed for 2027.
- Tax-credit education, license plates, raffle and sponsorship spotlights are optional inserts after current product eligibility, attribution, terms and any legal/tax claims are verified. No deadline or tax amount is assumed.

## Native dashboard plan
Website/Supabase owns team status, roster readiness, verified progress and approval. Mailchimp owns campaign delivery/suppression. Resend handles sign-in and operational messages.

Phase 1 (live): current early-access campaign status alongside actual sign-in/team/roster progress.
Phase 2: campaign library and calendar with draft/review/ready states, exact recipient preview, exclusions, duplicate-send protection and send history. These drafts have an empty recipient segment and no schedule.
Phase 3: consent-aware audience sync, suppression reconciliation, template rendering from current team data, and controlled reminders. Before automated sends, confirm offer/deadline facts, freshness, permissions and repeat-send prevention.

## Measurement
- Recruitment: eligible unique contacts → verified sign-ins → teams created → approved teams; count each once.
- Readiness: approved teams with >=6 participants and complete contact/shirt details; gaps by industry/liaison.
- Fundraising: verified net dollars; approved teams reaching $3,000; remaining gap; stretch-goal progress. Member credit is an attribution view of the same dollars.
- Messaging: provider sent/bounced/unsubscribed; opens/clicks are diagnostic only. UTM campaign IDs support analysis but do not prove causation.
- Weekly chairman review: invitees not started, pending approvals, missing rosters, below-goal teams, unresolved transactions, feedback.

## Release checklist
General signup or named cohort enabled; real links verified; event/date/deadlines confirmed; 2027 products active; fundraising tracking live before fundraising/standings sends; participant consent and suppressions checked; exact current recipients reviewed; no placeholder text; all drafts remain unscheduled until approved.
